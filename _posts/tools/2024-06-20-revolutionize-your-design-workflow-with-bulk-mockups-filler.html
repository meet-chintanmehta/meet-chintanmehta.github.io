How Bulk Mockups Filler Works:

  1. Prepare your PSD mockup template with at least one smart object layer.
  2. Organize your files into three folders: PSD mockup templates, design files, and an output folder for your generated mockups.
  3. Run the script and input parameters like folder paths, resizing options, alignment, and output options.
  4. Click "Make" and let Bulk Mockups Filler work its magic!
